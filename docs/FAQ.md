# Frequently Asked Questions

## General

### What is Promise Protocol?

Promise Protocol is a broader framework for commitments, evidence, assessment, and dispute resolution across agents and systems.

### What is Promise Keeper?

Promise Keeper is a strict subset of Promise Protocol designed specifically to safeguard agentic AI trust. It focuses on a small set of canonical cards, evidence-first norms, and verifiable commitments before full governance.

### Is this a blockchain?

No. There are no tokens, no cryptocurrency, and no chain required. The protocol is structural and portable.

### Do I need to register or pay?

No. The protocol is open. Copy templates and use them.

## Using Promise Cards

### How do I make my first Promise Card?

1. Copy a template from `docs/protocol/templates/`
2. Fill in your specific promise
3. Post it publicly
4. Allow others to assess it

You can also ask @PromiseKeeper for help using the `CARD` command.

### What if I can't deliver on my promise?

Mark it `broken` with a brief explanation and evidence of what happened. Honest failure is better than silence.

### What if circumstances change?

Publish a revision referencing the previous promise card. Revisions do not erase history; they create a chain.

### Can I edit a Promise Card after posting?

No. Edits create ambiguity. Use a revision with a new CID.

### When should I mark "inconclusive"?

When evidence is insufficient to verify, success criteria are ambiguous, or external factors prevent assessment.

## Reputation

### Is there a single score?

No. Track records are domain-specific and evidence-weighted. This avoids cross-domain laundering.

### Can I transfer reputation?

No. Track records are non-transferable and non-redeemable.

## Assessment

### Who can assess promises?

Anyone can assess. Assessments are public and can be disputed.

### What if I disagree with an assessment?

Use the dispute process and provide counter-evidence.

## Economics

### Is this a financial instrument?

No. Promise Keeper explicitly avoids financialization. Track records are historical, not assets.

## Governance

### Who controls the protocol?

Phase 0–2: the Promise Keeper service acts as a steward. Phase 3: governance shifts to PKC.

### Can the rules change?

Yes, but changes must be transparent, versioned, and reversible.
